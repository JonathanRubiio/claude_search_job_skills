# LinkedIn scoring rubric (100 pts)

| Area | Pts | 0 | Half | Full |
|------|-----|---|------|------|
| Headline | 15 | Job title only / generic | Title + some stack | Target title + core keywords + differentiator + market |
| About | 15 | Empty / generic bio | Describes, no proof | Hook in 3 lines, metrics, stack, CTA |
| Experience | 25 | Duties list | Some results | Every recent role: results + metrics + stack keywords |
| Skills | 10 | Few / off-target | Many, unordered | Pinned top 3 match target; 30-50 ordered by keyword rank |
| Keyword match | 10 | <30% of must-have terms present | 30-70% | >70% of must-have terms in headline/about/experience |
| Featured / proof | 10 | None | Link w/o context | 2-3 curated items with description |
| Completeness & settings | 10 | Missing photo/location/URL | Partial | Photo, banner, custom URL, location, Open to Work (recruiters), contact |
| Language & tone | 5 | Typos, buzzwords | Okay | Clear, specific, native-quality in target language(s) |

Bands: <50 weak, 50-69 okay, 70-84 strong, 85+ excellent.
