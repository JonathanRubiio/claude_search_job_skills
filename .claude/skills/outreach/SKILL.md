# outreach

Inputs: vacancy folder in `outputs/`, `data/profile.md`, tracker row. Optional: the target person's profile text (user pastes or read-only Chrome).

## Steps
1. **Pick the channel and person**: hiring manager > recruiter > team engineer (referral) > company generic. Find names from the posting, company page or the user's contacts. Never guess emails.
2. **Draft** by type (all in the language of the target person):
   - **Connection note** (<=200 chars): shared context + specific reason. No pitch.
   - **Recruiter/HM DM** (<=90 words): 1 line why them/the role, 1-2 lines of relevant proof (metric), clear ask (a 15-min chat / "I applied, happy to share more"), easy out.
   - **Referral ask** (to a contact): context, role link, why fit in 2 lines, make it effortless (attach CV, offer a blurb they can forward).
   - **Follow-up** (after 5-7 business days of silence): short bump adding one new piece of value; max 2 follow-ups, then stop.
   - **Thank-you** after an interview (within 24h): reference one specific topic discussed.
3. Provide 2 variants (direct / warmer) per message; flag any claim that needs confirmation.
4. Save in `outputs/<date>-<company>-<role>/outreach.md`; add `Next action` and `Due` to the tracker.

## Rules
- No mass or templated blasts; each message must reference something specific.
- Do not send or click anything in LinkedIn/email. The user copies and sends.
- No pressure tactics, no fake urgency, no false familiarity.
